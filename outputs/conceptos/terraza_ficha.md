## Resumen en español
- **Barbacoa (extremo del muro de block):**
  - los dos Weber contra el muro;
  - a su derecha, un carrito de servicio metálico negro con ruedas y un baúl de exterior verde oscuro con tapa para carbón y herramientas;
  - una vela de sombra color arena tensada desde las ménsulas negras del muro.
- **Comedor:** la mesa plegable larga con mantel blanco y las 6 sillas blancas, sobre el azulejo descubierto cerca de la parrilla, paralela a la casa.
- **Sala (centro, bajo el alero, mirando al césped):** la banca mecedora de madera y 2 sillas de exterior con brazos y cojines verde salvia, más una mesita auxiliar.
- **Rincón de los nietos:** una mesita infantil de plástico con 3 sillitas y una caja de juguetes con tapa, junto a la sala.
- **Fuera:** la manguera, la pelota medicinal, la pesa, la tela negra y las macetas sobre la mesa.
- **Se queda:** el azulejo (con antideslizante transparente, invisible en la foto), las orquídeas de la reja y las guirnaldas.

## Design spec (canonical, English)
At the grill end, the two black kettle barbecues stand side by side against the grey block wall; just to their right stand a black metal serving cart on lockable wheels and a dark green outdoor storage box with a lid. A taut sand-coloured triangular shade sail is stretched from the existing black metal brackets on the block wall. On the open tiles near the grills, parallel to the house, the long folding table is set with a plain white tablecloth and the six white folding chairs. In the middle of the terrace, under the roof overhang and facing the lawn, the wooden glider bench sits with two outdoor armchairs with armrests and sage-green cushions and a small side table; next to them is a children's corner with a small plastic kids' table, three little chairs and a lidded toy chest. The walkway along the house and the edge toward the lawn are completely clear. The hose, the medicine ball, the kettlebell, the black cloth and the potted plants on the table are gone. The orchids on the wrought-iron window grille stay, tidied into one row.
